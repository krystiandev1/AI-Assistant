## Product overview
CDQ Fraud Guard is a fraud prevention solution that helps companies protect against payment fraud.
It verifies bank accounts, provides trust scoring, and alerts on fraudulent activity.

## Bank Account Verification
CDQ Fraud Guard verifies bank account details to ensure payments reach the intended recipient.
It checks IBAN validity, account ownership, and flags suspicious account changes.

## Trust Score
The CDQ Trust Score assesses the risk level of business partners and transactions.
A high trust score indicates a reliable counterparty; a low score signals elevated fraud risk.

## Fraud Alerts
CDQ Fraud Guard provides real-time alerts when suspicious patterns are detected,
such as sudden bank account changes, unusual payment amounts, or known fraud indicators.

## Fraud Case Management
The solution includes tools for managing fraud cases, documenting incidents,
and tracking resolution status.
