# CDQ Fraud Guard

Source: https://www.cdq.com/products/cdq-fraud-guard

## Overview

Protect Your Business, Secure Your Transactions

In today's digital economy, payment fraud is an ever-present and growing threat to businesses of all
sizes. CDQ Fraud Guard is a comprehensive fraud prevention solution designed to help companies protect
themselves against payment fraud. By leveraging the power of community-shared data and advanced
verification techniques, CDQ Fraud Guard provides unparalleled protection against fraudulent
transactions.

## Combat Payment Fraud

CDQ Fraud Guard leverages the power of community-shared data to identify and mitigate payment fraud
before it impacts your business. By participating in the CDQ Data Sharing community, companies
contribute to and benefit from a collective intelligence network that continuously evolves to counter
new fraud tactics. This collaborative approach ensures that no business has to face payment fraud
alone, with shared insights providing early warnings of emerging threats.

## Protect Your Business from Payment Fraud

CDQ Fraud Guard integrates advanced verification capabilities and real-time fraud alerts to create
a multi-layered defence against payment fraud. The solution combines bank account verification with
trust scoring to provide a comprehensive risk assessment for every transaction. When suspicious
activity is detected, immediate alerts ensure your team can act quickly to prevent financial loss.

## Key Features

### Bank Account Verification

Utilises a shared database of validated bank accounts to verify payment details before transactions
are processed. CDQ Fraud Guard checks IBAN validity, confirms account ownership, and flags any
suspicious changes to bank account information. This proactive verification prevents fraudulent
redirections of payments to attacker-controlled accounts.

### Trust Score

Assigns a trust score to bank accounts and business partners based on historical transaction data
and community-reported fraud incidents. A high trust score indicates a reliable counterparty with
a track record of legitimate transactions, while a low score signals elevated fraud risk and
warrants additional scrutiny before proceeding with a payment.

### Payment Fraud Alerts

Warns community members about potential fraud attacks and suspicious activity in real time. When
CDQ Fraud Guard detects patterns consistent with known fraud tactics, such as sudden bank account
changes, unusual payment amounts, or activity matching known fraud indicators, it immediately
notifies affected businesses so they can take preventive action.

### Fraud Case Management

Allows users to document, manage, and look up fraud cases within the CDQ community. The fraud case
management module provides tools for recording fraud incidents, tracking the resolution status of
open cases, and searching for known fraudsters. By sharing case data with the community, businesses
contribute to collective fraud awareness and help protect others from the same actors.

### Seamless Integration

Easily integrates with existing financial systems and ERP platforms via a REST API, requiring no
dedicated user interface. CDQ Fraud Guard is designed to fit into existing workflows without
disruption, allowing businesses to add fraud protection capabilities to their current systems
within days rather than months.

## CDQ Fraud Guard Highlights

### Enhanced Security

Reduce the risk of fraud by verifying bank account data against a continuously updated shared

Streamline the process of verifying bank account data and reduce the manual workload on your

Allows customisation of trust scores based on your organisation's specific risk tolerance and

Leverages the power of the CDQ Data Sharing community to deliver fraud intelligence that no single
organisation could produce alone. Shared insights from thousands of verified business transactions

Provides immediate alerts on potential fraud cases the moment suspicious activity is detected,
